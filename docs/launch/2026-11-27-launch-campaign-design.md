# Launch campaign: Vault Deck Forge, HyMeta, Hustle & Flow (design)

Date: 2026-11-27. Choices marked "Ligia" are hers; *(Claude's call)* items are proposals she can change.

## Why
Three finished or nearly finished products are sitting unlaunched. Ligia wants them out and earning,
each with a social campaign, on a calendar, with Claude keeping her on top of every step.

## Goals
1. **Staggered launches** (Ligia), each tied to a moment its audience already cares about:
   - **Vault Deck Forge: Tuesday, December 8, 2026.** It is public and has no blockers. The holiday
     break is when Vault Hunters players have the most time. It also tests the posting system on the
     lowest-risk product.
   - **HyMeta: Wednesday, January 13, 2027,** the first birthday of Hytale's early access
     (Jan 13, 2026). Its ready playpacks run out after early February.
   - **Hustle & Flow: Tuesday, January 26, 2027 (estimate),** when tax filing usually opens. The IRS
     hasn't announced the 2027 date; last year it was Jan 26. The date moves to match once the IRS
     announces it in early January.
2. **Each launch has three phases** *(Claude's call)*:
   - Buildup, 2 to 3 weeks: teasers, a "coming soon" post, a launch date.
   - Launch day: an announcement on every channel, plus community posts.
   - Follow-through, 2 weeks: tips, features, replies to feedback, a "what's new" post.
3. **Posts go out automatically after Ligia approves them** (Ligia). Claude writes and schedules every
   post in a weekly batch. Ligia approves the batch with one tap, then each post goes out at its time,
   the same way the Lumina Instagram posting works today.
4. **Ligia stays on top of things without having to remember anything.**
   - A weekly push notification every Monday morning lists what's due that week (her errands and
     posts awaiting approval) and what happened last week.
   - A reminder goes out the day before each of her deadlines.
5. **Money:**
   - Vault Deck Forge stays free, with a small "support the tool" tip link (Ligia).
   - HyMeta: its decided membership, $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year.
   - Hustle & Flow: kept in its private repository.

## Update 2026-11-27: everything automatic (Ligia)
- **No approval step.** Posts go out on their own. Before a post is scheduled, a separate reviewer
  agent checks it against that product's rules (HyMeta: child-safe, "An unofficial fan site", no mod
  names, logos or numbers, a playpack only in its own week; Hustle & Flow: education, not advice, no
  overpromising; Vault Deck Forge: unofficial, non-affiliated) and that its link works. A post that
  fails is held, not posted, and listed in the Monday summary. The Monday push becomes a report of
  what went out and what's next.
- **Reddit and Discord are automatic too, carefully.** Post only where each community's own rules
  allow self-promotion (a weekly promo thread, a creators channel), at low frequency. Communities that
  forbid it are skipped. The rules are re-read before each post.
- **Ads.** HyMeta and Hustle & Flow use the ads their existing promo setups already make. Vault Deck
  Forge gets its own promo setup, built the same way.
- The standing-rule updates in each project now read: Claude may post launch content that passed the
  rules check; nothing else.

## Non-goals
- No paid ads in this first round. Organic posting first, then decide on ads with real numbers
  *(Claude's call)*.
- No influencer deals or press outreach in round one; ideas for those go in the follow-through phase.
- No changes to the products beyond what launch needs: the tip link, real launch URLs, and turning
  off private-test settings.

## Anti-goals (a failure even if it works)
- Anything posted that Ligia didn't approve.
- A launch day on which the product isn't actually open, or payments don't work.
- A post that breaks a product's own rules:
  - HyMeta: "An unofficial fan site" on every ad, no mod names, logos or numbers, and child-safe.
  - Hustle & Flow: education, not advice, and no overpromising.
  - Vault Deck Forge: unofficial, non-affiliated.
- Getting banned from a community (Reddit, Discord) for self-promotion.

## How it runs
- **One Google Sheet per product** *(Claude's call)*, so Hustle & Flow's confidentiality and each
  project's separation rules hold. Each product also connects its own social accounts. This master
  session keeps only a dates-and-errands view across the three. Each sheet has these tabs:
  - `errands`: what, product, due, status
  - `posts`: date, time, product, platform, caption, media, approved, status, link
  - `results`: weekly views, sign-ups, tips and sales per product
  - `change_log`
  
  Ligia approves posts by ticking "approved" in each product's sheet. The weekly push links all three.
- **Three scheduled tasks** *(Claude's call)*:
  1. **Weekly planner, Mondays.** Writes next week's posts with images from each product's promo
     folder, fills the Sheet, checks errands, pulls results, and sends one push: "5 posts ready to
     approve · 2 errands due this week · last week: 340 views, 12 sign-ups".
  2. **Poster, a few times a day.** Posts approved rows that are due (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube,
     Threads), writes the link back, and pushes Ligia only if something fails.
  3. **Deadline nudges, daily.** A push only when one of Ligia's errands is due tomorrow or overdue.
- Posting uses the Composio connection already working for Lumina's Instagram, with each product's
  own accounts. No new paid services.
- Images come from each product's existing promo folders (about 90 ads each for HyMeta and
  Hustle & Flow), plus new screenshots for Vault Deck Forge.
- **Every post must link to a page that works.** The planner checks the link before a post goes into
  the approval batch.

## Approaches considered
- **Chosen: staggered launches** (Ligia), each on its audience's moment, which also spreads the work.
- One big launch week: one splash, but the slowest product sets the date and the timing hooks are lost.
- Launch each when ready: no deadlines, so things tend to slip.

## Left to the builder
The Sheet's exact columns, the post copy, the batch sizes, the posting times per platform, and how
results are gathered (from platform analytics where a connection exists, otherwise by hand).
