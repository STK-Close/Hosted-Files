# Lumina Art House logo kit (2026-11-27)

The moth: drawn to light, the way people are drawn to our worlds. Designed with ChatGPT,
traced to clean vectors, made perfectly symmetrical, with the name set in Cinzel
(free font, SIL Open Font License; see CINZEL-FONT-LICENSE.txt).

Colors: gold #C9A06A · onyx #0B0908 · pearl #F9F3EF

## Rules
- Don't stretch, recolor outside the three colors, or add effects (shadows, glows).
- Leave clear space around the logo at least as wide as one moth wing star.
- The moth alone is fine wherever the name is already nearby.
